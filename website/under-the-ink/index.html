under the ink

a bit of red

i like how much you can tell about somebody’s taste from a few seconds of a cracktro. the demoscene pieces i was looking at made me want a little piece of my own for ring64. the red felt right early on. finding the movement took longer. i wanted the shapes to pull apart like they do in a lava lamp and spread like ink in water. something i could pass through and take a little of the red with me.

ink from scratch

every shape comes from two pigments on a grid. a small c program makes the first pattern and moves the pigment from one frame to the next. when i brush across it, the ink moves with the pointer and keeps curling after i stop. the renderer smooths the grid and paints the colors. a little paper grain sits over the canvas.

the little binary

the binary below is the x86-64 build of the c program. the browser needs an emulator to execute those instructions. ring64 translates them into webassembly as it runs the program. i could compile the same c straight to webassembly, too. i wanted the math that moves the ink to be something i could watch ring64 run.
