under the ink

where it began

i’d spent a lot of time measuring ring64. this time i wanted to make a little piece of art with it. the demoscene gave me the idea of letting a program make the whole image.

i kept thinking about ink in water and the way shapes in a lava lamp pull apart and meet again. i took ring64’s red and near-black and let them spread across cream. you can stir the ink just by passing through it. it keeps going when you move away.

from c to canvas

wet ink is a c program. i could compile the same source straight to webassembly for the browser. clang builds the version on this page as x86-64 machine code. running that file in a web page takes a cpu emulator. ring64 translates its instructions into webassembly as the program runs.

each frame, the browser passes the pointer position to the program. the program moves two layers of pigment across a grid. the renderer smooths the grid, gives the pigment its colors and paints it on the canvas.
