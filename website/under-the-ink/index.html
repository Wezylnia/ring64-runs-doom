under the ink

a folder of frames

i remember opening the folder for an early version of wet ink and seeing frames/. i’d been watching it thinking the ink was being drawn right there. around then i was trying out demoscene pieces on ring64. i liked that a little program could make a whole image, and that’s what i wanted for the ink.

i knew i wanted to keep the red and the cream. the movement took me longer to explain. i kept saying “lava lamp.” i meant the bit where a shape pulls apart and comes together again. i wanted the edges to wander like ink in water, so i could move through a patch of red and take a little of it with me.

what’s running

i wanted to hand ring64 a binary and see it move the ink. i build the c program for x86-64. a web page needs an emulator to run that file. ring64 translates its instructions into webassembly as it goes. i could compile the same c source straight to webassembly and run that version in the browser, too.

when i move the pointer, the page passes its position to the program. the program works out where the two layers of pigment go next. the renderer takes those values and paints what you see on the canvas.
